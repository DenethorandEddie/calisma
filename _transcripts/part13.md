- TWY A between A19 and A20 may be used as HLDG PSN for de-icing OPS on the J-APN. Avoid HLDG on the upslope between A19 and A20 to prevent unintentional backward movement of ACFT. High PWR settings may cause jet blast damage. Advise ATC if unable to comply with taxi CLR.

**Signboard PROC (CDF)**
- When instructed by GND, contact Padcontrol.
- When instructed by Padcontrol, contact Iceman with call sign and report additional requests (e.g. fuselage de-icing) if applicable.
- Iceman will instruct: ENTER (P10, P12, ...).
- Hold position, monitor Iceman and signboards for current treatment, start time and anti-icing code.
- Treatment is completed when anti-ice code is displayed on signboard. Cockpit preparations and flight control checks may now be performed.
- Hold PSN until STOP on signboard is extinguished. Iceman will advise when clear and to contact GND for taxi instructions.

**Voice only PROC**
- When instructed by GND, contact Padcontrol.

### 3.8 Noise Abatement Procedures (NAP)
Use ICAO Standard NADP 2. If unable to comply with NADP 2, inform DLV and request NADP 1. To reduce environmental burden, ACFT shall use as few ENG as possible whilst taxiing to RWY. Apply only when deemed safe and in accordance with company SOPs.

### 3.9 Low Visibility Procedures (LVP)
INT TKOFs are not allowed, except TKOFs from RWY 24 INT TWY S6 and S8 may be allowed at ATC discretion. If INT TWY S6 is in use, INT TWY S7E will not be used.

### 3.10 Warnings — 3.10.1 Caution Notes
There is a loss of line-up distance of at least 200m/656ft when choosing an intersection TKOF with intersection angle of 30° (rapid TWY) and the TWY center line is followed until the RWY center line.
