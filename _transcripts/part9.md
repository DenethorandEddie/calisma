## IMG_1300

**App header:** SXS-OMB - Operations Manual Part B - 1.8.2 Non-AFM Operational Inform... | Breadcrumb: Operational Information

**Page header:** 1.8.2 Non-AFM Operational Information — Page Revision Date: 12/Jan/2026

### 1.8.2. Non-AFM Operational Information

APU bleed valve must be closed when:

- ground air connected, and the isolation valve is open
- engine no. 1 bleed valve is open
- isolation and engine no. 2 bleed valves are open.

The APU bleed valve may be open during engine start, but avoid engine power above idle.

Allow a minimum of 30 seconds between failed APU start attempts. Up to three consecutive start attempts are permitted. After three consecutive failed start attempts, a 15-minute cooling period is required before further attempts.

Run the APU for two full minutes before using it as a bleed air source.

### 1.9.2. Non-AFM Operational Information

\# Do not deploy the speedbrakes in flight at radio altitudes less than 1,000 feet.

Alternate flap duty cycle:

- When extending or retracting flaps with the ALTERNATE FLAPS position switch, allow 15 seconds after releasing the ALTERNATE FLAPS position switch before moving the switch again to avoid damage to the alternate flap motor clutch
- After a complete extend/retract cycle, i.e., 0 to 15 and back to 0, allow 5 minutes cooling before attempting another extension.

### 8.4.1.5. Low Visibility Operation CAT II and CAT III Crew Briefing

A low visibility approach briefing shall include the low visibility operation checklist items.

The briefing should include the normal items as for any approach briefing. In addition the following subjects should be covered prior to the first approach:

- Crew qualification currency.
- Aircraft systems status and capacity.
- Destination and alternate weather.
- Airfield and runway operational status CAT II / CAT III, LVP.
- Assessment of landing performance calculated with the automatic landing option.
- Brief review of task sharing, brief review of procedure in case of malfunctions below 1000ft.
- SCCM is informed that a low visibility approach will be executed (usage of PEDs by passengers is not allowed).
