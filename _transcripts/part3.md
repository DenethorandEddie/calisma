### 3.9. Rejected Takeoff Maneuver
(heading in orange)

The Captain has the sole responsibility for the decision to reject the takeoff. Make the decision in time to start the rejected takeoff maneuver by V1. If the decision is to continue the takeoff, the Captain clearly announces "CONTINUE". If the decision is to reject the takeoff, the Captain clearly announces "STOP" immediately starts the rejected takeoff maneuver and assumes control of the airplane. If the First Officer is making the takeoff, the First Officer maintains control of the airplane until the Captain makes a positive input to the controls.

Before 80 knots, reject the takeoff for any of the following:
- activation of the master caution system
- system failure(s)
- unusual noise or vibration
- tire failure
- abnormally slow acceleration
- takeoff configuration warning
- fire or fire warning
- engine failure
- predictive windshear warning
- if a side window opens
- if the airplane is unsafe or unable to fly.

Above 80 knots and prior to V1, reject the takeoff for any of the following: [cut off]
