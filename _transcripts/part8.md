## IMG_1277

**App header:** SXS-OMB - Operations Manual Part B - 1.2.5.1 Taxiway Width and Taxi Spe[eds]

### 1.2.5.1. Taxiway Width and Taxi Speeds

Minimum width of Taxiway is 15 m.

Maximum RET* Taxi speed: 50kts when RWYCC is 6. Otherwise, 30kts.

Maximum Taxi Speed: 30kts.

Maximum Taxi Speed while crossing over a hook barrier**: 25 kts.

Maximum Taxi Speed in apron areas: 20kts.

Maximum Taxi Speed during turns with a turning angle >45°: 10kts.

> **NOTE**
>
> Taxi speed shall be adjusted according to runway, taxiway, and apron surface and weather conditions. If the Taxiway or Runway is contaminated, special attention shall be maintained.

> **NOTE**
>
> \* Rapid Exit Taxiways (RETs) are either defined in the AOI or RET lights are present in the AGC. RET lights shall be operative if higher than 30 kts is to be used at night.

> **CAUTION**
>
> Exceeding the RET speed limit may result in loss of directional control, increased stopping distance, or [run]way/taxiway excursion or incursion, especially on uneven [or c]ontaminated surfaces. [partly hidden behind back button]

The several changes to improve overall performance are;

- **Winglet lift credit (For Takeoff)**

  Higher takeoff weights or shorter takeoff field lengths Can also result in increase derate capability

- **Increased flight spoiler deflection on ground (For Takeoff and Landing)**

  Increases deflection to ~60° on the ground when used as speed brakes

  Increases deflection of the inboard ground spoilers from ~52° to ~60

- **Sealed slats for all takeoff flap positions**

- **Reduce idle thrust delay (For Landing)**

  Reduces the approach idle to ground idle transition delay time from 5 seconds to 2 seconds

- **Two-position tailskid (for Landing)**

  Provides body contact protection for landing conditions

  Allows higher approach attitudes, which result in lower Vapp speeds

### 1.4.1. Non-AFM Operational Information

With either one or both engine bleed air switches ON; do not operate the air conditioning packs in HIGH for takeoff, approach or landing.

> **NOTE**
>
> The fire protection Non-Normal procedures take precedence over the statement regarding no air conditioning pack in HIGH during takeoff, approach, or landing. The CARGO FIRE and SMOKE/ FUMES REMOVAL checklists require the operating PACK switch(es) HIGH. Switch(es) need to be placed in HIGH in order to increase ventilation for smoke removal.
