Who flies

OM-A 5.1.4.5.2: the FO is PM for takeoff when RVR is less than 200 m. R18L/0200N is exactly 200 m, so the FO may fly. If the RVR drops below 200 m before takeoff, the Captain takes it. Pick your role above.

Controls are split as in OM-B 3.10. PF: control column, thrust levers, CMD A, the calls. PM: gear, flaps, start levers, fire switches, MCP HDG SEL / bank / LVL CHG, fire warning cancel, stopwatch, ATC. Touching the other pilot's control counts as an error.

Flap bugs used here for 69.5 t: flaps 5 → 1 at 180 kt, 1 → UP at 200 kt, flaps-up speed 220 kt (approximate, read your own FMC).
