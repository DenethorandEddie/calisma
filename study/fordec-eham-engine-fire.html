DDECISION · What are we going to do?

Return and land EHAM RWY 06 · ILS CAT I · flaps 15 · no reverse · overweight landing

1. First priority, safety: EHAM 06 is the first acceptable option and the one with the least time in the air.  2. It is also the most efficient: home of the handling, maintenance and passenger solutions.

Go-around plan: if not stabilised by 1000 ft or no visual reference at DA → OEI go-around, then re-assess (RVR / wind); next option EHEH only if 06 becomes unusable.

Prepare

  • FMC: ILS 06, flaps 15 Vref (+ wind additive within the flap placard), missed approach, EO acceleration.
  • Charts / navaids: ILS 06, missed approach, MSA. Identify the ILS.
  • Cold temperature correction at −4 °C on DA and own-navigation altitudes (not under radar vectors).
  • Landing performance tool: actual weight, flaps 15, RWYCC 4, crosswind, no reverse, autobrake setting.
  • Overweight: normal profile, no high sink rate, land in the touchdown zone, full length, no early turn-off, no autoland (OM-A 8.3.1.19.1, FCTM).

Briefing (TEM)

  • Threats: fire, crosswind at the limit, contaminated runway, overweight, flaps 15, manual thrust, falling visibility, snow on the windshield.
  • Who lands (Commander decides), callouts, stabilised criteria, OEI go-around, evacuation plan after stop.
