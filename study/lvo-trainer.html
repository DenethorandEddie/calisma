Rules the scenarios test
  • FO role: in LVO the Captain is PF and you are PM (OM-A 5.1.4.5.2). You watch head-down to roll-out, call deviations and failures, and if the Captain does not respond you initiate the go-around. Any malfunction after the Captain goes head-up: you call "GO AROUND" (OM-A 8.4.1.4).
  • CAT II and III need a dual-channel automatic approach and landing, flaps 30 or 40, both engines operating, an operative EFIS control panel, and a runway at least 45 m wide (OM-B 1.5.1, 1.5.3.1).
  • Autoland wind limits, gusts included: headwind 25 kt, crosswind 10 kt, tailwind 10 kt (OM-B 1.5.1).
  • Stabilized approach (OM-A 8.3.1.16.6): stable by 1000 ft, landing flaps set, speed within target +10 / −5 kt. Deviation limits: CAT I 1 dot, CAT II/III ½ dot on the localizer and the glideslope.
  • Approach ban: if the controlling RVR is below minima before 1000 ft above the aerodrome, do not continue. Below 1000 ft you may continue to DH (EASA CAT.OP.MPA.305; the OM-A page is not in the screenshots).
  • At DH: no required visual reference means a go-around.
